Ten ways to show Calories

Landing page directions for vite-hub/calories, the ViteHub Agent template that turns a meal photo, voice note, or message into calories and protein, saved to a database you choose.

Each page is one self-contained HTML file. Every visual is drawn in code. Pages 5, 7, and 10 use shaders.com WebGPU shaders when your browser has WebGPU, and hand-written WebGL2 otherwise.

    Not linked anywhere. Estimates in these pages are example values.
